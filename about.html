Why RP Organics Exists

Bringing Traditional South Indian Health Foods to Every Home

RP Organics was born from a simple belief: the food our grandmothers made — poondu podi, millet kanji, sundakkai kulambu, pirandai chutney — was not just delicious but deeply nourishing. We wanted to bring that wisdom back to modern kitchens.

RP Organics was born with a singular resolve: To build an uncompromising grocery brand that never cuts corners. We work directly with smallholder farming clusters in Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya for pure Lakadong turmeric, Karnataka for unpolished millets, and Saurashtra for traditional wood-pressed oils.
