Shipping & Returns Policy

1. Nationwide Shipping Policy

At RP Organics, we prioritize the freshness and purity of your food. Every order is freshly packed from small batches milled at our farm facilities and dispatched within 24 business hours.

  • Ordering via WhatsApp: Customers browse products, select pack sizes and quantities, then click “Order on WhatsApp” (or send their full Order List). Delivery addresses are shared directly in the WhatsApp chat, where stock availability and final payment arrangements are confirmed.

  • Notify our team within 7 days of receiving the package at benseethalrani@gmail.com or WhatsApp us at 9150300641 with your Order ID.

Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to warehouse dispatch. Once the parcel is in transit, you may simply refuse delivery or request an immediate return upon arrival.
