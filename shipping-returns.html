Shipping & Returns Policy

1. Nationwide Shipping Policy

At RP Organics, we prioritize the freshness and purity of your food. Every order is freshly packed from small batches milled at our farm facilities and dispatched within 24 business hours.

  • Free Shipping: Available on all orders with a cart value equal to or greater than ₹999 across all serviceable pin codes in India.
  • Standard Shipping Fee: For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping charge of ₹99 is added at checkout to cover protective eco-friendly packaging.
  • Courier Partners: We dispatch exclusively via express air couriers including Bluedart, Delhivery, DTDC, and Express Post.
  • Estimated Delivery Timelines:
    • Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata): 2 to 3 business days.
    • Rest of India: 3 to 5 business days.

2. 7-Day Organic Purity & Taste Guarantee

We stand 100% behind the authentic taste, aroma, and certified organic quality of our products. If you receive an item that is damaged in transit, past its optimal freshness, or fails to meet your quality expectations, you are entitled to our 7-Day No-Questions-Asked Resolution.

  • Notify our team within 7 days of receiving the package at benseethalrani@gmail.com or WhatsApp us at 9150300641 with your Order ID.
  • We will immediately arrange a free reverse pickup or send an express replacement batch.
  • Refunds are credited to your original payment method (Bank, UPI, or Card) within 3 to 5 business days upon pickup confirmation.

3. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to warehouse dispatch. Once the parcel is in transit, you may simply refuse delivery or request an immediate return upon arrival.
