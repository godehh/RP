Spring Physics • Interactive Fan

2. <BounceCards />

Features realistic 240Hz spring physics simulation. Cards dynamically fan out, push neighboring cards apart on hover, straighten upright, and respond with tactile overshoot pops on click!

👉 Hover over any card to trigger spring spread, or click a card!
